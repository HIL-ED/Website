Paris, 1919

The Treaty of Versailles

The Great War is over. Delegations from the United States, France, and Great Britain have gathered outside Paris to decide the terms Germany must accept. Each nation wants something different — and none of them can get it alone.

You will lead one delegation through seven real sticking points from the actual 1919 conference: war guilt, reparations, the German military, colonies, the Rhineland, Alsace-Lorraine, and the League of Nations. Every issue is decided by a vote of all three powers. Get two votes, and it's in the treaty.
