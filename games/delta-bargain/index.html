The Delta's Bargain

A Land, Sea & Livelihood Simulation · AP Human Geography, Unit 5 (Topics 5.9–5.11)

Welcome to the Amara Delta

The Amara Delta is a fictional river-mouth region modeled on real deltas around the world — places like the Mekong Delta, the Niger Delta, and coastal Ecuador — where a rainforest and mangrove forest meet rich coastal fishing grounds. For generations, local families have farmed, fished, and lived off this land and water.

You've just been named the Delta's new Development Coordinator. Global markets want what the delta produces: shrimp, timber, palm oil, fish. Over the next 10 years, you'll make the calls that decide whether the delta thrives, or collapses under the weight of its own bargain.

🌳

Land Health

Forest cover, soil quality, and the ecosystems the delta depends on.

👥

Community Wellbeing

Local jobs, food security, and whether families can stay and thrive.

💰

Profit

The money you need to invest, govern, and keep the delta running.

Every decision moves all three. Let any one collapse to zero, and the whole system fails — no matter how well the other two are doing. Choices are tagged with the AP Human Geography concepts they demonstrate, so you're building exam-ready vocabulary as you play.

How to play

Each round presents a real decision development officers like you actually face — drawn straight from the environmental and economic issues in CED Topics 5.9 (The Global System of Agriculture), 5.10 (Consequences of Agricultural Practices), and 5.11 (Challenges of Contemporary Agriculture). Pick a choice, see its effect on all three meters, and read why it matters before moving to the next year. Some crises only appear if your earlier choices set them up — the delta remembers.

Plays in about 20–30 minutes. Grab a partner and talk through the trade-offs out loud — there's rarely a clean right answer.
